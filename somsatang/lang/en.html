Directions to the Accommodation

🚌 By Public Transportation

  • Bus: From the Suwon Station Yeokjeon Market (수원역.역전시장) stop, take bus #35 or #7-1, get off at Janganmun (장안문), then walk about 8 minutes (approx. 30 min).
  • Taxi: Approximately 10-15 minutes from Suwon Station (about 4km).

🚗 By Car

  • Enter the address "15-6, Suwoncheon-ro 406beon-gil, Jangan-gu, Suwon-si, Gyeonggi-do" into your navigation system.
  • Parking: Public parking lots (open 24 hours, max ₩7,000 per day)
    1. Yeonmu-dong Public Parking (연무동공영주차장) — 57, Gyeongsu-daero 743beon-gil, Jangan-gu, Suwon-si
    2. Hwahongmun Public Parking (화홍문공영주차장) — 280, Paldal-ro, Jangan-gu, Suwon-si

✈️ By Airport Bus

  • Take airport bus #4000 from Incheon Airport, get off at Hanil Town (한일타운), then about 10 minutes by taxi.
  • Boarding stops (tap to show on the map)
    📍 Terminal 1, 1F Stop 8A
    📍 Terminal 2, B1 Stop 36
    Approx. 1.5 hours.
